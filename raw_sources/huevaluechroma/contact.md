# CONTACT About David Briggs

David Briggs has been teaching drawing and colour theory since 1996, at Sydney's Julian Ashton Art School (1996-2020), as a sessional lecturer and instructor at the National Art School, Sydney since 2009 and as a sessional lecturer at the University of Technology, Sydney since 2018. A page detailing his public courses is located [here](https://sites.google.com/site/djcbriggs/classes). Other significant teaching positions have included six years as the life drawing instructor for Disney in Australia (2000-2006), and lecturer in drawing at Sydney's Billy Blue School of Graphic Arts (2006 - 2010). His publications include a chapter in the *Routledge Handbook of Philosophy of Colour*. David is Vice President, Past President and NSW Divisional Chair of the Colour Society of Australia, and is a committee member of the [International Colour Association (AIC) / Inter-Society Color Council (ISCC) *Colour Literacy Project*](https://color.printing.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Maggio-Color-Literacy-Project.pdf), an international educational initiative to provide age-appropriate colour curricula and state-of-the-art resources for teachers across science, art and industry. David has been teaching practical classes and workshops on colour for painters for 20 years and has also written and presented an elective at Sydney's National Art School on the history of colour theory and practice. He gave an account of his unique approach to colour education as an invited speaker and workshop presenter at the [ISCC/AIC Munsell Centennial Symposium](https://iscc.org/Munsell-Centennial-Presentations) in Boston in 2018. David obtained a Ph. D. in Geology and a Bachelor of Science (Hons, First Class) at the University of Queensland. A selection of his artwork may be viewed online at [here](https://sites.google.com/site/djcbriggs/artwork).

The main body of *The Dimensions of Colour* largely dates from early 2017 or earlier. My more recent work, including that presented in the ["Afterthoughts" section](http://www.huevaluechroma.com/111.php) at the end of this site, is indexed separately or is currently only available as resources for students of my public online course through the National Art School, Sydney, [here,](1114.php)*[Understanding and Applying Colour](https://sites.google.com/site/djcbriggs/tmct)*.
