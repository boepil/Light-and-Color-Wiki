# 11.14 Index of Publicly Available Works

![](pics/1114.jpg)

Collected below are links to most of my publicly accessible musings related to colour, apart from discussions on forums and the like. The list is limited to publicly available links, and so excludes resources available only to students of [*Understanding and Applying Colour*](https://sites.google.com/site/djcbriggs/tmct), most reports for the [Colour Society of Australia](https://coloursociety.org.au/) and its Members' newsletter *e-Spectrum*, peer reviews for journals including [*Color Research and Application*](https://publons.com/journal/25995/color-research-application/), and presentations for which there are no published recordings or slides.
