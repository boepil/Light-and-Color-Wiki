MIX TO FIND OUT
Each pigment's hue, value, and purity are fixed together as a set, the way a real tube of paint is — you don't get to dial Ultramarine any lighter than Ultramarine actually is. White and black are true flat, spectrally neutral reflectors, not just very pale or very dark colors. The only continuous control is how much of each pigment goes into the mix. Real complements rarely cancel to a perfectly neutral gray — getting the spread under ~35 is a genuinely good result. Worth trying: mix a good complementary pair, then add white and watch that it lightens things without actually pulling you closer to gray.
