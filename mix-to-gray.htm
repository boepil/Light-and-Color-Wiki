MIX TO FIND OUT
Each pigment's hue, value, and purity are fixed together, the way a real tube of paint is — you don't get to dial Ultramarine any lighter than Ultramarine actually is. The only continuous control is how much of each pigment goes into the mix. Real complements rarely cancel to a perfectly neutral gray either — getting the spread under ~35 is a genuinely good result.
