# Wave Nature

**Scope:** Details the physical wave nature of light, covering wavelength, frequency, and spectral power distributions.

## Synthesized Content

Light is a form of **electromagnetic radiation**, specifically coupled electric and magnetic fields that oscillate transversally (perpendicularly) to their direction of travel. 

*   **Wave-Particle Dualism:** Light possesses properties of both waves and particles. In the wave model, it is a vibrating disturbance spread in space. In the particle model, it consists of discrete, indivisible packets of energy called **photons** or corpuscles.
    *   **"The Science of Paintings":** "We can describe light as electromagnetic waves with color identified by its wavelength. We can also consider light as a stream of minute packets of energy called photons, which create a pulsating electromagnetic disturbance."
    *   **"color-for-science-art-and-technology.pdf":** "quantum theory, explains the equivalence of the particle and wave characteristics of light... A photon or quantum may appear to act in two apparently very different ways."
*   **Universal Speed:** In a vacuum, light travels at a constant universal speed ($c$) of approximately **300,000 kilometers per second** or 186,000 miles per second.
    *   **"vision-science-photons-to-pheno":** "Photons radiate outward from their source... at the enormous speed of 186,000 miles per second."
    *   **"Illusions of Seeing":** "This wave propagates from the source with the very high speed of light, constant for the vacuum, of approximately 300,000 km/s."

Light is physically quantified by its wavelength, frequency, and photon energy.

*   **Wavelength ($\lambda$):** The physical distance between one wave crest and the next, typically measured in **nanometers (nm)** ($10^{-9}$ meters) for the visible region.
    *   **"The Science of Paintings":** "The wavelength is the distance from one wave crest to the next, and is represented by the Greek letter lambda, $\lambda$."
*   **Frequency ($f$ or $\nu$):** The number of waves passing a stationary observer per second, measured in **Hertz (Hz)**. 
    *   **"The Science of Paintings":** "Moving waves can be described by their frequency, or number of waves per second... the range of the pure spectrum perceived by the eye extends from about $4.3 \times 10^{14}$ Hz in the red range to about $7.5 \times 10^{14}$ Hz in the violet."
*   **Quantization of Energy ($E$):** According to Planck’s Law, the energy of a photon is directly proportional to its frequency and inversely proportional to its wavelength ($E = hf$). In a vacuum, energy in electron volts (eV) relates to wavelength as **$E(eV) = 1240 / \lambda(nm)$**.
    *   **"The Science of Paintings":** "There is an inverse relation between the energy $E$ of the photon and the wavelength... In terms of wavelength in nanometers (nm) and energy $E$ in electron volts (eV)... $E (eV) = 1240 / \lambda (nm)$."
*   **Physical Amplitude:** The height of the light wave determines its physical intensity or flux (the number of photons passing through a unit area in a unit time).
    *   **"The Science of Paintings":** "The intensity or brightness of the light is defined by the flux, which is the number of photons passing through a unit area in a unit time."

## Handprint Perspectives

Bruce MacEvoy (Handprint) emphasizes a fundamental conceptual shift when discussing the physics of light: "color is in the mind, not in the light." Wavelengths of electromagnetic radiation possess no inherent color; they are purely physical phenomena of energy. The perception of color only arises when this energy interacts with the biological mechanisms of the human eye. 

This strict separation between physical stimulus and biological response is critical. By understanding that wave nature is purely physical, we can better contextualize the [[The Visible Spectrum]] and recognize why perceptual models like [[CIE Systems]] must be introduced to translate raw energy into human color experience. *(Source: [[raw_sources/handprint/color18a.md|color18a.html]])*
