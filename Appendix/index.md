### What this section is about

The main wiki sections teach **Light**, **Eye**, **Colors**, **Painting**, and **Pigments**. The appendix sits beside them — not another topic to master, but the **support layer**: where sources live, how the history fits together, how color is measured in the lab, and (for builders) notes on visualizing color in 3D.

You do not need to read the appendix cover to cover. Most visitors need **Bibliography** only when tracking down a source, or **History & Key Figures** when they want the big chronological story. The other pages are there when you need them.

### The pages, one by one

#### [[Bibliography|Appendix/Bibliography]] — find any source the wiki uses

The complete list of books, papers, videos, and web resources, grouped by **Light / Eye / Colors / Painting**, plus Handprint and HueValueChroma blocks. Every entry is *"Title" — Author*; page numbers appear on the individual wiki pages where a claim is made. **If you liked one page and want the book behind it, start here.**

#### [[History & Key Figures|Appendix/History & Key Figures]] — one story from cave ochre to modern pigments

Not a list of biographies — a **single narrative** about how art, pigment-making, and color science kept pushing each other forward: prehistoric walls, Vermeer and the synthetic boom, Impressionist tubes outdoors, Chevreul and Munsell, today's forensic pigment dating. Written for anyone who wants **the historical thread** that ties the whole wiki together. Read it straight through, or jump to the era you care about.

#### [[Data & Methodology|Appendix/Data & Methodology]] — how color is measured and verified

The technical back room: spectrophotometers, sampling protocols, the standard calculation pipeline that turns a reflectance curve into CIE numbers, classic datasets (Munsell Renotation, Pointer's gamut, MacAdam limits), and the caveats (observer variation, bandpass width, fluorescence). **For readers who want to know how the numbers on the Colors pages are produced** — or how to trust a measurement claim.

#### [[Project Notes|Appendix/Project Notes]] — notes for building a 3D color-space viewer

Working design notes for an interactive tool — which solids to render (CIELAB, Munsell tree, gamut hulls), coordinate conventions, and navigation tricks (constant-lightness slices, hue wedges). **Not required reading for understanding color**; it documents a planned visualization layer on top of the wiki's content.
