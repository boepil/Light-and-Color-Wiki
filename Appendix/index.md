### What this section is about

The main wiki sections teach **Light**, **Eye**, **Colors**, **Painting**, and **Pigments**. The appendix sits beside them — not another topic to master, but the **support layer**: where sources live and how the history fits together.

You do not need to read the appendix cover to cover. Most visitors need **Bibliography** only when tracking down a source, or **History & Key Figures** when they want the big chronological story. The other pages are there when you need them.

### The pages, one by one

#### [[Bibliography]] — find any source the wiki uses

The complete list of books, papers, videos, and web resources, grouped by **Light / Eye / Colors / Painting**, plus Handprint and HueValueChroma blocks. Every entry is *"Title" — Author*; page numbers appear on the individual wiki pages where a claim is made. **If you liked one page and want the book behind it, start here.**

#### [[History & Key Figures]] — one story from cave ochre to modern pigments

Not a list of biographies — a **single narrative** about how art, pigment-making, and color science kept pushing each other forward: prehistoric walls, Vermeer and the synthetic boom, Impressionist tubes outdoors, Chevreul and Munsell, today's forensic pigment dating. Written for anyone who wants **the historical thread** that ties the whole wiki together. Read it straight through, or jump to the era you care about.

### How the pieces fit together

The appendix does not duplicate the teaching sections — it **anchors** them. History links outward to [[Painting/index|Painting]], [[Pigments/index|Pigments]], and [[Colors/index|Colors]].
