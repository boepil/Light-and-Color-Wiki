**Scope:** The project's meta-layer — how this wiki is built, where every claim traces back to, and the working notes behind the corpus. Includes the unified citation map, the query-and-verify methodology, the historical arc of color science, and the project log.

### [[Bibliography|Appendix/Bibliography]]
The unified citation map of the entire wiki: every book, paper, video, and web resource, grouped **Light / Eye / Colors / Painting** plus a **Handprint (web sources)** block. Entries follow the convention *"Title" — Author* with no year/edition/publisher; page-level citations appear on the individual wiki pages where they are used.

### [[Data & Methodology|Appendix/Data & Methodology]]
Documents the wiki's grounding chain: the **NotebookLM notebook "Color Light and Painting"** as the authoritative source of truth, the handprint.com and huevaluechroma.com raw-source mirrors in `raw_sources/`, and the direct-query rule — every page is populated by querying the notebook (never from local dumps), with each claim tied to a named source and gaps explicitly flagged.

### [[History & Key Figures|Appendix/History & Key Figures]]
The historical arc of color science: from Newton's prism and Goethe's phenomenology through Maxwell's color matching, Helmholtz and Young, Chevreul's simultaneous contrast, Munsell's notation, the CIE standardization, and the opponent-process physiology of Hering's descendants.

### [[Project Notes|Appendix/Project Notes]]
Working notes and conventions: the page anatomy template, citation conventions, the sequence-numbering system that drives the sidebar order, tracker files (`page-status.md`, `index.md`, `log.md`), and the image-banner prompt style used for header plates.
